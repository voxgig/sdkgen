  Main findings:
  (1) The documentation is not one kind of thing.
  - Four families are mostly restatements of facts the shared description already needs: ReadmeModel and ReadmeEntity restate the REFERENCE, ReadmeTopQuick restates the quick start, and ReadmeIntro is one paragraph. The proposal fits them strongly.
  - Two families are mostly documents about one language: ReadmeExplanation (0% of units shared by 15 or more languages, 50% found in one language only, 54% of words in one language only) and ReadmeInstall (0% shared, 81% of units and 86% of words in one language only).   - ReadmeOptions (1,206 lines) renders nothing in any of the 20 languages (D26). It is deletion, not sharing.

  (3) Two shared components with a language table already exist (ReadmeErrors, ReadmeExplanation). Both print the TypeScript row for 15 of the 20 languages (S05/X1). Their apparent 81-95% sharing is that defect.
  
  (4) The same facts are stated in many families. The error convention appears in 10 places, the data accessor in 10, the test client in 4, apikey in 5. A migration has to move them together, by fact, or each document contradicts itself as stage 1 found.
