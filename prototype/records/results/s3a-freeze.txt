    ts/test/runtimefacts.test.ts drives a check(t, Syntax, forms) for every provider found in the staged scaffold, against the forms each language declares in ts/test/runtimeforms/<lang>.ts. It reads only the files the forms name (client, entity and ops sources, the generator components included).

    - facts.method, each defined as an operation;
