general_or_not: The proposal solves part of this catalogue; it is not a general solution. Its mechanism reaches about one entry in five (66 of 329, 50 of 263 distinct), and the prototype tested about one in eight. Six of the tested entries were not fixed, because their component was shared before the prototype began. A quarter of the catalogue is already written once (O). Writing per-language families once does nothing more for it. What it needs is the other half of the design: single exported derivations such as targetSurface, targetPlace, docLink, featureOnByDefault and modelCopyMode. 40 distinct defects depend on these. Just over half the catalogue is consumer Mains, generated code and tests, the generator's tools, docgen, create-sdkgen, apidef and the packs. A documentation structure cannot touch any of that, and those defects stay individual fixes in about 35 of the 46 packages. The design principle that does span the whole catalogue is the one the proposal's first rule restates: one resolved answer per fact, read by documents, code generators, consumers and docgen alike. Writing sentences once is the documentation half of that principle, and it is worth doing for the README families: it removes WP29 and WP31 and shrinks nine more packages.
